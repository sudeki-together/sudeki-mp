# Despawn cue

- Duration: 260 ms
- Waveform: two blended triangle tones
- Main sweep: 780 Hz down to 260 Hz
- Shimmer sweep: 1170 Hz down to 480 Hz
- Envelope: linear fade to silence
- Source: original SudekiMP procedural audio (`src/cleanroom/audio.c`)

The short descending sweep is intended to read as a clean digital dissolve.
